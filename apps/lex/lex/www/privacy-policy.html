Data protection

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Lexocrates handles personal data across our website, client portal, payment journey and professional-services operations.

Effective: 25 September 2026Last updated: 25 September 2026Applies to website, portal and services

1. Data controller and scope

Lexocrates Legal Services Private Limited (“Lexocrates”, “we”, “us” or “our”), operating from Floor 1, E-103, Ganpati Enclave, Sirsi Road, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302041, India, determines how personal data is used for its website, client portal, contracting, billing and service administration. For Client Materials processed solely on a Client’s documented instructions, the Client may be the data controller and Lexocrates may act as its processor or service provider.

We process personal data in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as brought into force and applicable, and contractual safeguards agreed for cross-border engagements. References to international privacy standards describe our operational approach and do not claim that every foreign statute applies to every engagement.

2. Information we collect

  • Identity and business contact details, including name, organisation, designation, business email and telephone number.
  • Billing and transaction information, including billing address, country, tax identifiers, invoice details, transaction currency, gateway order/payment references and payment status.
  • Account information, including portal role, authentication events, access logs, security preferences and support history.
  • Engagement and Client Materials, including instructions, uploaded documents, communications, approvals and Deliverables.
  • Technical information, including IP address, device/browser information, timestamps, cookie identifiers, diagnostic logs and security events.
  • Compliance information reasonably required for conflicts, sanctions, fraud prevention, accounting, taxation, RBI reporting or lawful regulatory requests.

We ask Clients not to provide personal data that is unnecessary for the agreed Services, particularly sensitive personal data, privileged material or third-party data for which they lack authority.

3. Payment-card security

We do not store or process complete credit/debit card numbers or CVV on our servers. All transactions are securely handled via RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

Payment gateways and issuing banks independently process card credentials, authentication, fraud screening, tokenisation and settlement. We receive limited transaction metadata such as a gateway order ID, payment ID, amount, currency, method, masked card details, status and failure reason. A gateway may use the email or phone entered at checkout for customer verification and saved-card access, subject to its own privacy notices and consent flow.

4. Purposes and lawful use

We use personal data to respond to enquiries; verify and administer accounts; perform conflicts and security checks; prepare quotations and SOWs; deliver and quality-review Services; provide support; generate invoices and accounting records; process and reconcile payments, refunds and disputes; prevent fraud; maintain audit trails; enforce agreements; protect systems; and meet legal obligations.

For international receipts, information may be used for banking, foreign-inward-remittance, tax, GST, RBI purpose-code, FIRA/eFIRC and statutory reporting. Depending on context, processing is based on consent, performance of a contract, compliance with law, protection from fraud and security threats, or other grounds recognised by applicable law.

5. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal data. We disclose it only as reasonably necessary to:

  • payment gateways, banks, card networks, accounting and fraud-prevention providers;
  • cloud hosting, email, document, security, analytics and business-software providers acting under contractual restrictions;
  • professional advisers, auditors and insurers subject to confidentiality duties;
  • courts, regulators, tax authorities, law enforcement or other authorities when legally required; and
  • a successor in a lawful merger, financing, reorganisation or transfer, with appropriate safeguards.

Because our Clients and service providers may operate in different countries, data may be accessed or processed outside the individual’s country. We use contractual, access-control and security measures appropriate to the engagement and applicable transfer requirements.

6. Cookies and tracking technologies

We use strictly necessary cookies for login, session security, CSRF protection, preferences and load balancing. We may use limited analytics to understand aggregate website performance and improve navigation. Non-essential cookies should be used only where permitted and, where required, after consent. Browser controls can block cookies, but disabling necessary cookies may prevent portal authentication or payment checkout from working.

7. Retention and security

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, active engagement, dispute limitation period, contractual requirement, secure backup cycle and applicable professional, accounting, tax or regulatory retention obligation. When retention is no longer required, data is deleted, anonymised or securely isolated, subject to technically necessary backup rotation.

Safeguards include role-based access, authentication controls, encryption in transit, controlled storage, audit logging, malware screening, least-privilege administration and incident-response procedures. No internet service is completely risk-free, and Clients must also protect credentials, devices and authorised-user access.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and verified identity, an individual may request information about processing, access to personal data, correction or updating, deletion of data no longer required, withdrawal of consent, restriction or objection, and grievance redressal. Some requests may be limited where retention or disclosure is required by law, legal privilege, security, fraud prevention, establishment of legal claims or another person’s rights.

Send a request to support@lexocrates.com with the subject “Privacy Rights Request”. We may request proportionate information to verify identity and authority before acting.
