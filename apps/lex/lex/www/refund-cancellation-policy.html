Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains when a digital professional-service order may be cancelled or refunded and how domestic and international card refunds are processed.

Effective: 25 September 2026Last updated: 25 September 2026CAD · USD · INR

1. Scope and nature of services

This policy applies to payments made to Lexocrates Legal Services Private Limited for legal process outsourcing, legal operations support, document-based assignments, custom workflows and prepaid legal capacity. Our Services and Deliverables are digital and professional in nature; there are no physical goods to return.

An accepted SOW may contain project-specific cancellation or refund terms. Those terms prevail for that engagement where they are more specific and do not reduce rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

2. Cancellation before commencement

A Client may request cancellation before substantive work or a paid milestone has commenced by emailing support@lexocrates.com. Include the Client name, order or invoice number, payment ID and reason. We will confirm whether work, resource reservation, licensing or third-party commitment has begun.

If no substantive work has commenced and no non-cancellable cost has been incurred, the eligible payment will be refunded to the original payment source. For milestone engagements, cancellation applies prospectively: completed or commenced milestones remain payable, while an uncommenced prepaid milestone may be refundable after permitted deductions.

Ongoing engagements may also be terminated in accordance with the applicable SOW and the 30-day notice provision in our Terms & Conditions.

3. Refund eligibility

A full or proportionate refund may be approved where:

  • Lexocrates does not commence or deliver the purchased Service and no alternative delivery arrangement is accepted;
  • an agreed material milestone is not delivered after written notice and a reasonable opportunity to cure;
  • the same order is accidentally charged more than once;
  • the amount captured materially exceeds the authorised order amount;
  • Lexocrates cancels the unperformed portion of an engagement without Client breach; or
  • a refund is otherwise required by applicable law or expressly approved in writing.

A quality concern must identify the affected requirement and be raised during the review period in the SOW, or within seven business days of delivery if no period is stated. We may first correct or re-perform a deficient Deliverable where that is commercially reasonable.

4. Non-refundable items

Except where required by law or caused by our uncured material breach, the following are non-refundable:

  • completed or accepted custom legal-support, document-review, research, drafting, software, automation or workflow milestones;
  • time already spent and resources already committed to an active milestone;
  • third-party licences, databases, filing charges, specialist fees, taxes or other non-recoverable expenses approved by the Client;
  • expedited or urgent-work surcharges after priority capacity has been reserved;
  • used, consumed or reserved prepaid legal capacity associated with completed work;
  • delays or rework caused by incomplete instructions, inaccurate Client Materials, scope changes or missed Client approvals; and
  • issuer, intermediary-bank or foreign-exchange fees not received by Lexocrates.

5. Refund processing timeline

  1. We acknowledge a complete refund request within 24 business hours.
  2. After eligibility is approved, we initiate the refund with the payment gateway within 24 to 48 business hours.
  3. After gateway initiation, the credit ordinarily appears on the cardholder’s bank statement within 5 to 7 business days, including the normal international clearing cycle.

The 5–7 business-day period is controlled by the gateway, card network and issuing bank and is therefore an estimate rather than a guarantee. Public holidays, compliance reviews, expired/replaced cards, intermediary processing or cross-border banking may take longer. We will provide the available refund reference so the cardholder can trace it with the issuing bank.

6. Original source, currency and foreign exchange

Refunds are issued only to the original payment source and ordinarily in the original transaction currency. We do not redirect a card refund to another card, person or bank account merely on request. If the original source is unavailable, the gateway and applicable compliance requirements determine the permitted alternative.

The cardholder’s bank controls the exchange rate used when the original payment and refund are converted. Accordingly, the statement credit in the cardholder’s home currency may differ from the original debit. Lexocrates is not responsible for issuer foreign-exchange spreads, cross-border charges or currency movements that it did not receive. Gateway fees will be handled according to the gateway rules and the applicable SOW.

7. Resolution before a chargeback

Before initiating a card chargeback, please contact support@lexocrates.com or submit our contact form. Provide the order/invoice number, payment date, amount, currency, masked card last four digits, reason and supporting documents. Never send a full card number, CVV or OTP.

We will acknowledge the dispute within 24 business hours, review gateway and delivery records, and propose a correction, re-performance, cancellation or eligible refund. This process does not remove any non-waivable cardholder right, but it gives both parties an opportunity to resolve mistakes faster and avoid unnecessary card-network proceedings.

8. How to submit a request

Email support@lexocrates.com from the registered billing email or use the Contact Us page. Requests are handled Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST, excluding public holidays.
